The goal is to check off the next TODO box, not to complete the project.
You don't know where the project will end, and that possibility/unknown is the fuel.
If you try to make something of it all the fuel source becomes completion.
However, great things are never complete, they grow and adapt forever.
So if you are building something great and your goal is completion you will surely run out of fuel.
If you want to build great things let the unknown fuel you, and focus on simply the next step.
