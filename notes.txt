
PACKET SYNCHRONIZATION::
    SENDING
        when writing to a socket from a file.
        this need FS to read to and send to NET
            but you dont want to overload NET
            so NET needs to notify FS of the successful chunk
            then FS can reuse that same buffer...
        Maybe you can do even two chunks at once...

    RECIEVING
        FS notifies NET of failed write
        NET then has to mark some boolean as false.
        from there no more chunks relating to that file are sent.
        and an error message is sent to sender notifying them of it.
